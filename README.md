## 📌 Project Overview & Mobility Context
**MRA IRELAND — Official Motorsport Website UI/UX Clone & Redesign** is a Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) web usability evaluation and interface prototyping project completed at **INTI International University (Nilai, Malaysia)** during the **June 2026 Session** as part of a **Fully Funded International Student Mobility Scholarship Program**.

Supervised by **Kala Devi Managuran** in the Faculty of Data Science and Information Technology (assessing **CLO2: Construct prototype interfaces of computer systems and various websites based on HCI design principles**), this study investigates the live website of the **Motorcycle Racing Association (Ireland) Ltd (`https://www.mraireland.com/`)**—the official governing body for off-road motorcycle racing disciplines (**Motocross, Quadcross, Sidecarcross, Enduro, Supermoto, and Trials**) across all **9 Counties of Ulster**.

Representing affiliated clubs that host roughly **50 annual events** for **~1,500 members** and **700–800 competition licence holders**, the legacy Wix-built website suffers from deep multi-level dropdown menus, fragmented race schedules, inconsistent button affordances, and poor visual hierarchy. To modernize the platform while preserving its complete domain content, our team conducted comparative motorsport benchmarking, evaluated the portal against **Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics**, and engineered a comprehensive **15-screen Web UI Prototype in Figma**.

---

## ⚠️ Problem Justification: Legacy Website Usability Deficiencies
Governing body portals in competitive motorsport must provide racers, officials, and spectators with rapid access to race dates, entry forms, championship standings, and safety regulations. Our heuristic inspection and comparative analysis of the live `mraireland.com` website revealed six critical usability breakdowns:

* **🗂️ Deep, Text-Heavy Multi-Level Navigation:** Championship tables, competitor rulebooks, and coaching resources are buried inside nested dropdown menus, forcing users to execute multiple clicks and memorize menu paths to reach high-priority pages.
* **📅 Fragmented & Inconsistent Event Information:** Upcoming fixtures are duplicated across a basic homepage list and a separate Events Calendar page with inconsistent detail levels—some entries list venues and host clubs, while others display bare `TBC` placeholders that prevent rapid visual scanning.
* **📰 Poor Homepage Typography & Visual Hierarchy:** News posts, fixture rows, and footer sponsor strips compete equally for visual attention with minimal typographic contrast or whitespace, obscuring the single most time-critical element for riders: the **Next Race** countdown and round details.
* **🔍 Absence of Search & Category Filtering:** The legacy interface provides no search bar or category filter controls, forcing competitors looking for a specific youth class, grading list, or FIM policy PDF to manually browse the entire site hierarchy.
* **🔘 Inconsistent Interactive Affordances:** Crucial calls-to-action such as `Enter Online` and `Download PDF` are inconsistently styled as plain inline text links vs. buttons, reducing clickability recognition on both desktop and mobile viewports.
* **🔗 Unannounced External Form Redirects:** Event entry links abruptly route competitors to third-party forms (`Jotform` and `Google Forms`) without contextual labels or pre-redirect notices, creating trust and orientation friction for first-time participants.

## 🎯 Target Users & 15-Screen Information Architecture

### 👥 Primary User Segments
1. **Active Competitors & Riders (Youth & Adult):** Require immediate access to upcoming round dates, `Enter Online` links, transponder requirements, grading lists, number plate colour rules, and post-round PDF championship standings.
2. **Affiliated Club Officials & Race Organisers:** Reference the MRA Rule Book, FIM concussion guidelines, safeguarding/anti-doping policies, and licensing eligibility criteria to enforce regulatory compliance across Ulster clubs.
3. **Spectators, Families & Motorsport Fans:** Browse the website to check weekend race dates, track venues across the 9 counties of Ulster, general association news, and championship results.
4. **Prospective Riders & Commercial Sponsors:** Explore introductory coaching sessions, one-event vs. annual race licence fees, and series sponsorship opportunities.

## ✅ Heuristic Justification of Prototype Design

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 Click to Expand: Complete Evaluation Across All 10 of Jakob Nielsen's Usability Heuristics</b></summary>
<br>

| # | Nielsen Usability Heuristic | Legacy Website Issue (`mraireland.com`) | Redesigned Figma Prototype Solution |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **#1** | **Visibility of System Status** | Upcoming race details were buried in unstructured lists without clear temporal priority. | Highlighted **Events** sidebar on the Homepage (`1.jpg`) and structured **Events Calendar** (`2.jpg`) showing exact date, category, venue, and registration status before clicking. |
| **#2** | **Match Between System and the Real World** | Information was scattered across generic menu sub-items that did not mirror how riders prepare for race weekends. | Organized using authentic motorsport terminology (`Adult Solo`, `Youth Solo`, `Quad & Sidecar`, `Grading Lists`, `Number Plate Specifications`, `Transponders`, and `Medical Repatriation Cover`). |
| **#3** | **User Control and Freedom** | Deep nested dropdowns trapped users in isolated sub-pages with no clear way to pivot across sections. | Persistent 7-item top navigation bar (`Home`, `Events Calendar`, `Championship Info & Standings`, `Clubs`, `Competitor Info`, `Coaching`, `About`) on all 15 screens for instant one-click switching. |
| **#4** | **Consistency and Standards** | Links, tables, and championship pages varied wildly in structure, button styling, and alignment. | Standardized 2-column template across all 5 Championship screens (`6.jpg`–`10.jpg`) and uniform royal-blue action buttons (`Enter Online`, `Download PDF`) across the entire portal. |
| **#5** | **Error Prevention** | Cramped dropdown links and mixed event tables caused users to open the wrong championship or outdated document. | Segmented each championship into its own dedicated page, added calendar filter controls (`2.jpg`), and added version tags (`2026-V1`, `Last Updated February 2026`) on document cards (`11.jpg`, `13.jpg`). |
| **#6** | **Recognition Rather Than Recall** | Users had to remember which sub-menu held specific policies, plate specs, or grading PDFs. | Exposed all primary categories in the visible top navbar, added visual thumbnail cards for `Grading Lists` (`11.jpg`), and placed a comprehensive quick-link grid in the Homepage footer (`1.jpg`). |
| **#7** | **Flexibility and Efficiency of Use** | Both first-time visitors and veteran racers had to scroll through long unindexed lists to find their class. | Added direct quick-access buttons in the Homepage footer (`1.jpg`) and category/month filters on the `Events Calendar` (`2.jpg`) so experienced riders can jump straight to their discipline. |
| **#8** | **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design** | Cluttered homepage where news, events, and sponsor banners competed without whitespace or visual hierarchy. | Clean royal-blue (`#0047AB`), dark slate, and white brand palette with generous card spacing, clear `>>` section headers, and visual separation between primary race info and secondary sponsor links. |
| **#9** | **Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors** | Lack of prominent page headers made it difficult to realize when an incorrect sub-page had been opened. | High-contrast blue title banners (`>> Youth Solo Championship`, `>> Policies`, `>> Race Licences`) at the top of every screen (`1.jpg`–`15.jpg`) paired with persistent global navigation for immediate recovery. |
| **#10** | **Help and Documentation** | Critical regulatory rules, licensing fees, and start-permission contacts were hard to locate quickly. | Dedicated, scannable documentation screens for `Number Plate Specifications` (`12.jpg`), `Policies` (`13.jpg`), `Racing Outside Ireland` (`14.jpg`), and `Race Licences` (`15.jpg`). |

</details>
